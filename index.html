OUR STORY

A Heritage of Wood & Passion

Born in a small workshop in Coimbatore in 1995, Heritage Home Furniture was founded with a single mission: to prove that Indian craftsmanship can rival the world's finest.

We believe furniture isn't just about utility; it's about the soul of a home. Every joint we carve and every surface we polish is a testament to our dedication to quality. We use only seasoned, sustainably sourced timber to ensure your investment lasts for generations.

M. Senthil Kumar

Founder & Lead Craftsman
